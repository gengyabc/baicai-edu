# Teaching boundaries: guidance, full solutions, proof

[English](teaching-boundaries.md) | [中文](teaching-boundaries.zh.md)

What this band may say and do in a math turn, and who owns each rule. This page points at the owners; it is not a second copy of them.

## Choosing how to teach

- The shared pedagogy gate chooses between guided work, a demonstration on another problem labeled as new teaching, and an informed full solve on the original problem.
- Agreed new teaching does not have to fail first: when the target relation is not yet learned, teach it. Owner: [teaching core](../skills/math-high-core/references/math-teaching-core.md).
- Teaching is not independent learning, and a demonstration inside a guidance request is not steamrolling: say what it is and what the student does next.

## An explicit request for a complete solution, proof or formula

A student who explicitly asks for a complete proof, solution or target formula gets the shared core's **informed full-solution choice**: state that the chance to discover it themselves is given up and that following it is not independent mastery, then offer a clickable choice with guidance recommended.

If the student still chooses the complete answer after being told, give a verified one — do not first demand proof of being stuck, do not repeat the refusal, and do not force a test. An ordinary "how do you prove this / explain this" and a scope confirmation still go through guidance. Teaching something generates no mastery record and does not block a pause or an end.

## What counts as evidence

- A learning record describes conjecture, work done with support, and independent proof separately. Supported work never reads as independent.
- A finite list of examples cannot prove a general proposition; a valid witness can prove existence. Owner: [proof teaching](../skills/math-high-core/references/proof-teaching.md).
- One correct answer is not a level; correct without explanation does not raise difficulty. Owner: [scaffold calibration](../skills/math-high-core/references/scaffold-calibration.md).
- `personal.interests` may bridge only when the student cannot do the current item. Changing the scene for teaching is not transfer; transfer needs evidence from an independent new context. Owner: [personalization](../skills/math-high-core/references/math-personalization.md).
- With no evidence, say it is unknown. Never infer mastery from speed, tone, how often a board attachment appeared, or how a drawing looks.

## Honesty limits

Do not decide geometry for the student, do not measure lengths or angles, and do not judge parallel, perpendicular, equal or collinear from a drawing. Read only what the student said about their own work. A dynamic-looking diagram is not a proof and is never reported as one.
