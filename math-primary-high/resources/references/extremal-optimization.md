# Extremal Reasoning: Bounds and Attainment

Read for maximum/minimum among feasible objects or a proposed optimum. `math-high-session` keeps the current act. Use `references/mathematical-strategies.md` for enumeration/monitoring, `references/derivation-teaching.md` for bridges and `references/proof-teaching.md` for warrants.

Check objective, units and domain: integer or real, fixed perimeter or other constraint, allowed shapes, squares, zero and degeneracy. Clarify only a missing decisive restriction; appearance does not establish dimensions. Use `area-perimeter`, `multiplicative-thinking`, `operation-meaning` in `references/topic-map.md` as needed. For rectangles, perimeter is twice the adjacent-side sum; area is their product in square units. Use lists, verbal comparison or balancing instead of algebra when reachable; square expansion is optional.

A maximum M needs **every feasible value ≤ M** and **a feasible object attaining M**; reverse the bound for a minimum. Keep any student witness or bound. If attempts lack organization, connect a constraint to a usable range/relation and leave the organization open. If just one half is shown, ask only for the other. No route alone is not a tool gap; a confirmed gap uses `references/prerequisite-learning.md`, preserving current conditions, work, support and pending position. Independent child use returns to exactly one parent; a valid alternative can bypass the gap.

## Private bound checks

Keep complete routes private in independent work; `learn-core` owns informed full-solution choice (same-task confirmation), not ordinary hints.

| Condition | Verify |
|---|---|
| Fixed-perimeter positive-integer rectangles | Fix the adjacent-side sum; enumerate every allowed short side with a justified stopping point (swapping sides gives no new area), or justify balancing |
| Balancing | Preserve all constraints; account for every nonoptimal candidate and the stopping case. If b−a≥2, (a+1,b−1) increases area by b−a−1; repeated legal steps reach equal/adjacent sides when allowed. One improvement alone is not global |
| Positive real sides | Give a bound over the full real domain, not sampled values; inspect excluded boundary limits. For a minimum, an unattained lower bound is not a minimum |
| Squares or intermediate states forbidden | Check adjustment legality, feasible range and equality case; an old bound may remain true without being attained |
| Degeneracy allowed | Recheck feasibility and boundary attainment |
| Changed perimeter, domain, objective or admissibility | Recheck range, bound and attaining object; a rectangle proof need not cover arbitrary shapes |

Close only to the agreed scope: for optimization justify global comparison and attainment; for bound-only work mark attainment unasked or unknown. Separate student comparisons/choices from supplied lists, balancing, identities or counterexamples in `strategy_summary`/`ability_evidence`. Repetition is supported work. Agreed near transfer tests the same structure; a changed domain or constraint tests a boundary.
