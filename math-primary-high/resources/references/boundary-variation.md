# Boundary Variation: One Load-Bearing Condition Changes

Read for an agreed test of why a method works, a neighboring case changing a decisive condition, or reuse of an old reason after a change. `references/problem-design.md` owns task design, `references/mathematical-strategies.md` the methods and monitoring, `references/math-teaching-core.md` method selection; `math-high-session` retains the act and dispatch (no new leaf).

## Isolate the dependency

From the student's explanation, identify the fact a step needs to produce the intended object: counting unit, order/role, replacement change, repeat/generation rule, start, fixed total or allowed range. Change that fact alone, keeping other conditions and valid reasoning. If two changes are inseparable, simplify or treat it as a new task. Seek agreement first; offer near transfer instead if the goal is a familiar route on new material.

| Student response | Next move |
|---|---|
| Reuses the old reason | Ask which step the changed fact touches; leave its repair open |
| Says the problem is impossible | Check a candidate; failure of the route is not an exclusion of all solutions |
| Keeps a valid step or finds another route | Verify and continue it; neither a new witness nor the same answer vindicates the old route |
| Gives a new count without a reason | Ask what correspondence or recurrence covers every object |
| Sees no change or misunderstands the terms | Compare one counted object under both conditions |

State what survives, which dependency changed, what needs repair, and what remains open. Supply a replacement only via `references/scaffold-calibration.md`, labeled as help. A changed case may be easier and can still have solutions. Use `references/proof-teaching.md` for scope/direction and `references/early-algebra.md` for recurrence; a finite prefix versus a repeat belongs to the methods file.

## Private verification

Keep checked routes out of independent openings. `learn-core`'s informed full-solution choice (`references/informed-full-solution-choice.md`) owns the warning and same-task confirmation; a hint request does not authorize disclosure. No prerequisite study or follow-up exercise is required once confirmed.

| Changed condition | Check |
|---|---|
| Order or roles | Define the object and correspondence; any deduplication factor works for every object, not one sample |
| Starting state, same transition | Check start and requested range; retain the recurrence. New starting size alone is usually near transfer |
| Increment or generation action | Re-derive transition and pairing/coverage; distinguish recurrence from construction steps |
| Per-item contribution becomes variable | Find where constant replacement change fails; no solution needs separate proof; check a new route against all conditions |
| Fixed-contribution target | Check divisibility of discrepancy and allowed integer range; exclude every allowed count before claiming no solution |
| Number or assignment of categories | Recheck exhaustive assignment and total capacity before any guarantee |

## Evidence and completion

- **Boundary discrimination:** student names the changed condition, affected step and why the original route does or does not survive. A value alone or a teacher-supplied link is not independent evidence.
- **Near transfer:** same structure, new surface; success does not establish awareness of the limit.
- **Supported work / unknown:** attribute prompts and corrections; mark refused, unfinished or unclear checks unknown without lowering judgment. Return to the original task or stop when the student declines.

Use `references/math-segment-fields.md` for one merged segment; teacher-only explanation, choice or plan gives `session_segment: null`. For a confirmed gap, `references/prerequisite-learning.md` owns descent and one-layer return; carry both cases, conditions, pending reasoning and hint attribution.
