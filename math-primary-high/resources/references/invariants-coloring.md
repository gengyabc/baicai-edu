## Validate the invariant and its direction

Define the quantity, check its initial value and prove preservation for every legal operation type. In tiling, count uncovered cells; removing one of each color leaves the difference unchanged. A conflicting target value rules it out. Matching values say only that this test did not exclude the target: a necessary condition is not sufficient. Possibility needs a checked construction or separate sufficient proof.

## Private verification

Use `learn-core`'s informed full-solution choice for a complete-proof request (same-task warning/confirmation; no prerequisite or compulsory exercise). Keep routes private in independent work.

- Check initial/target classes and each legal orientation's contribution generally; sampled failures alone cannot exclude all sequences.
- A covering must use exactly the required cells with no gaps or overlaps. To refute sufficiency, use a counterexample meeting every premise, including the claimed region class; balance does not imply adjacency.
- If size changes, recount classes and retain valid preservation. Recheck every new contribution if legal pieces change: diagonal neighbors have row-plus-column sums differing by 0 or 2, so removing them changes black-minus-white by ±2. Losing the obstruction leaves reachability open.

Close at the agreed proof, repair or construction; distinguish student reasoning from supplied quantity and supported counts, and leave untested selection/transfer unknown. In optional extensions, respect refusal, pause, end and task changes.
