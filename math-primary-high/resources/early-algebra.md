# Early Algebra (CGI Relational Thinking and Generalization)

Read for `equality-variable`, `patterns-generalize` (`references/topic-map.md`), or `beginning_symbolic_relations`: meaning of `=`, compensation, operation claims, varying quantities, or generated old/new parts and proposed continuations. Build from the student's arithmetic; a letter names a quantity already understood.

## Directions and moves

Choose one direction per task step; these are revisitable, not a content gate.

| Direction | Task → evidence |
|---|---|
| Relational thinking | Coordinated changes in an open equality → explains how one side constrains the other |
| Equivalence | Vary result position in open or true/false sentences → judges from the relation, not just computation |
| Generalized arithmetic | Student conjecture → scope, counterexample search (e.g. swapping subtraction terms), covering reason; letters optional |
| Variable | Small case → far case → letter → names quantity and explains each term |
| Functional thinking | Two varying quantities in different representations → explains dependence and maps the same relation across them |

Pose structural tasks with computable numbers; observe, compare actual strategies and extend one step per turn per `references/math-teaching-core.md`. Accept computation without requiring a switch; ask about its details first. Invite and verify student-made cases. Keep strategy names out of assessment; name them in instruction after the idea is encountered. Demonstrate formal equation manipulation only on explicit request, under the core's labeled instruction and reveal rules.

## Structural recurrence

Separate conjecture from counting under a given generation rule and proving a general count. From a finite prefix the next term is **not uniquely determined**; state the assumed rule, and accept another justified continuation. See `references/mathematical-strategies.md` for the same boundary in cycles.

Establish start size/count, state sufficient for the next step, retained/added/removed parts without double counting, and a recurrence valid throughout the allowed range. A structural reason can suffice without formal induction. For a numeric target accept a justified stepwise count. A closed form (direct count without prior counts) is a separate goal, not a condition of recurrence. Use the **Formula-goal gate** and `references/derivation-teaching.md` for general formulas; `references/proof-teaching.md` for full-scope checks; `references/prerequisite-learning.md` for confirmed gaps and single-parent return. Shared reveal/exit rules apply; no compulsory transfer or formula.

- Small drawings but no count → compare adjacent sizes; leave the increment/count open.
- Viable stepwise route → keep it, check start and exact counting; length alone is not error.
- Correct expression without reason → ask what terms count or why the pairing covers the object; substitutions alone aren't proof.

At a real decision let the student judge what their attempt established and whether the rule is given or conjectured. Label and explain teacher-supplied joining/pairing; later substitution, division or repetition is supported execution, not discovery or monitoring.

### Private structural checks

Keep worked answers out of an independent opening; show only the permitted bridge.

- Stepwise: Initial size/count, exact increment, no recounting, coverage through the requested size.
- Joined copies: Every part in every copy counted once; justify each factor and division.
- New setting: seek student-made old/new connection, not a recalled quotient.
- Changed sharing, start or increment: identify the changed condition, revise and recheck coverage.

## Boundaries with Other Methods

Own arithmetic structure. Variation/contrast compares relational changes across operations; use inquiry/proof for other pattern and geometric claims, Pólya for unfamiliar multi-step problems even with an unknown. Select one primary method.

## Minimal contrasts

Reuse evidence per `references/strategy-diagnosis-rules.md`; choose one probe, not a fixed pair. “Answer comes next” → reverse result position or unknowns on both sides. “Examples prove” → ask if cases cover scope; out-of-scope is not a counterexample. “Letter labels an object” → ask its value in one case and what a term counts. “Increasing means proportional” → contrast constant ratio with a nonzero fixed part.

## Evidence and stage

Record task/topic, observed reasoning, stated rule/scope and linked representations in `strategy_summary`. For recurrence distinguish samples, conjecture, justified stepwise reasoning and closed form. Separate teacher supply and supported work in the single segment; no student performance from teacher demonstration alone. Map `symbolic_strategy_stage` per `references/progression-map.md`:

- `arithmetic_only`: computes sides/cases without using a relation.
- `relational_unknown`: uses the equality relation to judge/find an unknown, or solves one unknown by substitution/equation.
- `generalized_structure`: justifies a general relation over stated scope or links representations of the same relation.

Generalization explains what varies, what stays and why it extends, in any representation. Credit only shown scope. Examples alone, trial-and-error values or a symbol do not raise stage; hinted work leaves independence unknown.
