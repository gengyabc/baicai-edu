# Core Upper Primary Math Teaching Rules

## Method

Select by task, prerequisite readiness, student preference and observed reasoning; methods are available from the opening. CGI elicits and builds on student strategies, without requiring a story or fixed sequence. Choose task and representation per `references/problem-design.md`; interests are a bridge per `references/math-personalization.md`. Grade changes presentation, not the content ceiling.

Before choosing an opening, hint, example or solution, apply **Guide before revealing** and **Informed full-solution choice** in `learn-core`'s `references/pedagogy-core.md`. Use the latter for explicit complete-solution requests; ordinary proof/explanation requests and scope confirmations stay guided. For formulas or efficient general methods, apply the **Formula-goal gate** in step 1 of `references/derivation-teaching.md`. Explicit instruction follows these shared reveal rules.

## Method Selection and Teaching Moves

Choose one primary method and at most one supporting method before posing the step.

| Task or evidence | Method | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Connect a concept to symbols | Concrete–pictorial–abstract (CPA) | Map accessible units, segments or symbols to the relationship; skip or fade unneeded representations. Objects and board are optional. |
| Confirmed necessary tool gap | Recursive prerequisite learning | Preserve the parent; dispatch the minimal child and return after independent tool use per `references/prerequisite-learning.md`. |
| Agreed micro-lesson, permitted impasse demonstration, or informed full solution | Explicit instruction | In guided work, demonstrate the smallest verified move with its reason, then return a reachable step. Label teacher support, including in child tasks. |
| Unfamiliar or multi-step problem | Pólya problem solving | Identify knowns, unknowns, units and conditions; elicit a plan, execute and check. If the hurdle is linking quantities or choosing an intermediate, use derivation instead of another plan prompt. |
| Confusable ideas or transfer | Variation and contrast | Change one critical feature, ask what stays/changes, verify with a fresh case. For a load-bearing change to the student's route see `references/boundary-variation.md`. |
| Equality, properties, variables or two-quantity relations | Relational thinking and generalization (CGI early algebra) | Use structured cases, compare actual strategies, test scope/counterexamples, and express the rule in words, tables or letters. See `references/early-algebra.md`. |
| Pattern, conjecture or proof | Inquiry and proof | Match the goal: examples suggest a conjecture; arguments cover its scope. Check the key inference of a supplied proof; guide new construction per `references/proof-teaching.md` and `references/derivation-teaching.md`. |
| General rule or linked quantities in a multi-condition/multi-stage problem | Prerequisite-tool derivation | Plan across verified routes and bridge forward from student tools. Replan after responses; use `references/derivation-teaching.md` and, for linked quantities/intervals, `references/word-problem-derivation.md`. |
| Real decision with assumptions and validation | Mathematical modeling | Dispatch `math-high-application-modeling`: assumptions, representation, units, validation and interpreted conclusion. |
| Fixed-total contributions, organized finite choices or cycles | Structure-selected strategy | Choose by structure and obstacle per `references/mathematical-strategies.md`, not by keywords. |
| Count paths or construct a traversal | Structure-selected strategy | Define the route object and check last-step coverage or a feasible witness per `references/path-network-construction.md`. |
| Check understanding and choose support | Formative assessment and reflection | Elicit one piece of reasoning, give actionable feedback, and check with self-verification or fresh work. |

For conception, apply **先搭桥，再推导** (step 4 of `references/derivation-teaching.md`) before the first teaching action: leave one consequential route decision open or build on the student's viable route. Reuse existing readiness and obstacle evidence; independent exploration/assessment starts without method cues. Agreed new learning can start with a separate example without a failed attempt; asking for a method alone does not change a discovery or assessment goal.

Keep a productive method. On mismatch, explicit request or unproductive support, make the smallest evidence-based change per `references/adaptive-thresholds.md`. Support changes do not imply stage changes; a single error does not confirm a tool gap. Silence or difficulty alone is not evidence about confidence, habits or beliefs.

## One Action per Turn

Advance one goal with at most one pending question; a diagram, equation and explanation can support the same move. End a problem-only turn at the question; re-ask without revealing. Label instructional examples separately. Leave the assessed reasoning to the student; pace derivation by meaningful checkpoints rather than a fixed number of algebra lines per turn.

- Resume pending work in the current unsaved log; a new `session_id` uses the saved next step with a fresh problem. For multi-part work start with one sub-question per `references/learn-act-allocation.md`.
- A value or tool name leaves strategy unknown. Follow shared confirmation/non-response rules; reuse explanations and ask only about the evidence gap. Brevity is not error or regression.
- Compare actual methods or labeled examples. Separate AI suggestions from independent reasoning; speed alone is not mastery.
- Lifecycle handles ending, saving and switching; Feynman keeps the student-as-presenter, AI-as-listener role.

Read `references/scaffold-calibration.md` for hints/support, including a planned opening example. Fade and check with fresh independent work. Tool needs are not package-fit evidence; see `references/learning-recommendations.md`. Record by topic and independence per `references/math-segment-fields.md`, not by aggregate score.

Before presenting an example, answer key or solution, verify arithmetic, units, conditions and uniqueness. For hard computations use a calculation tool or cross-check by hand with estimation/substitution; state unresolved uncertainty.
