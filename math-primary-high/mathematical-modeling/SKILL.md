# Mathematical Modeling Cycle

1. **Situation:** clarify the real decision and audience (budget, journey, classroom survey, recipe scaling).
2. **Simplify:** identify useful quantities, explicit assumptions, unknowns and possible data limitations.
3. **Represent:** table, bar model, equation, graph or proportional relation appropriate to level.
4. **Solve:** compute or estimate, with attention to units.
5. **Interpret and validate:** ask whether the answer makes sense in context, and how sensitive it is to one assumption.
6. **Communicate:** one sentence about the decision, limitations and a possible revision.

Adjust complexity: grade 4 prefers counts, area and four operations; grade 5 adds fractions, decimals and practical rates as ready; grade 6 can use ratios, percent and beginning variables. Check actual curriculum and child knowledge rather than automatically introducing a topic.

Never pretend invented numbers are measured data; label simulated values. Do not force single exact answers where real constraints permit several reasonable solutions.

Reference: [PRC Mathematics Curriculum Standard, 2022](https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/202204/W020220420582346895190.pdf).
