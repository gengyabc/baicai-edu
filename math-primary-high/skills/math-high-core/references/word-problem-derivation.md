# Deriving Relationships in Word Problems

Use `references/derivation-teaching.md` plus this quantity bridge when linked conditions/stages require an intermediate quantity or a reason for an equation or method (e.g. journeys, work, ratios, allocation). Use `references/math-teaching-core.md` and `references/scaffold-calibration.md` in the current act.

## Retain the goal

A fully specified textbook word problem stays in its current opening or practice act. A story, several steps or a check does not by itself trigger real-world modeling; that leaf requires an agreed real decision with assumptions, data choices or a model. Justifying a quantity relation does not automatically require a formal proof; read `references/proof-teaching.md` when proof is the goal.

Start ordinary help with one purposeful bridge without a formula/discovery menu. Use **Guide before revealing** in `learn-core`'s `references/pedagogy-core.md`; an explicit complete-solution request uses **Informed full-solution choice**, while local hints/explanations keep guided work. Use the Formula-goal gate only when discovering or learning a general method, not applying known relations.

## Map and bridge

Privately verify data, target, units and constraints. Name each quantity **object → interval → meaning → unit** (clock reading, elapsed time, moving time, total with stops). Ask for just the decisive clarification if conditions conflict or are missing; do not assume uniform speed, simultaneous departure or uninterrupted motion.

Work backward privately from the target to the nearest missing relation; verify feasible equation, decomposition, timeline or table routes. An intermediate quantity earns its place by linking known conditions to the target. Explain the clue/purpose, not just “set x” or “subtract speeds”; leave a substantive choice or inference. **Pose → observe → interpret → bridge** with one coherent thinking task and one pending question. Follow valid student alternatives; repair selection rationale before further arithmetic. Confirmed gaps enter a session-dispatched child per `references/prerequisite-learning.md`; return one layer after independent tool use.

## Interval-sensitive route checks

| Trigger | Bridge and check |
|---|---|
| Delayed departure or stop | Link target to a known distance–speed–time relation; leave representation/intermediate open. If no connection, offer a shared event state as a route hint and attribute it; reuse known states. Check head start versus simultaneous motion. |
| Meeting or pursuit | From known separation, investigate change over a common interval; check positions, directions, speeds and whether it closes. Equal speeds with a positive gap, or insufficient closing speed, may mean no catch. Accept a valid equation route too. |
| Event time given | Identify its time origin. Equivalent event times with different origins can both be valid; translate to the requested origin. |
| Rate change, turn or stop | Split at each relevant event, preserve endpoint states and locate the solution interval. Check event order and reconcile moving/elapsed time, distances and work totals. |
| Several workers or stages | Connect completed and remaining work in common units; sum rates only for justified combinations of active workers on each interval. |
| Different ratio bases | Name each whole/unit and express amounts against a common reference. |

Validate units, signs, totals, intervals and original conditions, not just the equation. A root outside the assumed interval fails that branch. Keep the verified full route private under the shared reveal rules. A chain of answer-bearing hints is still a supplied solution; ensuing arithmetic is supported execution.

## Evidence and continuation

Independent solving shows why the student chose the intermediate relation, how it leads to the target, and a check against the task. A quotient alone is insufficient. Permitted demonstrations/informed solutions need no forced exit test; teacher-only work is not student evidence.

Keep the current act/leaf owner and lifecycle rules. In `context.turn_state` retain task, time origin/interval, established relations, bridge, support and pending question. Separate student selections, teacher-supplied bridges, execution and checks in `strategy_summary` per `references/math-segment-fields.md`; no new persistent fields, submode or stage. Tool recovery uses the distinct optional plan.
