## Private guarantee checks

Keep complete routes private in independent work; use `learn-core`'s informed full-solution choice for complete-proof requests.

| Claim | Required warrant |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed at least k | Denial puts **every** category at at most k−1; total capacity must be strictly below the object count. Justify coverage of all assignments, not trials. Sum actual bounds if unequal; equality alone gives no contradiction |
| Tight threshold | Also exhibit an allowed assignment with maximum k; it refutes a stronger guarantee, not a proof of the lower bound |
| A larger group is impossible | Check a separate upper bound; the lower guarantee alone never supplies it |
| Identify group, members or actual size | Use actual assignment data and a checked grouping/search. A large group may differ across assignments; hypothetical distributions are not actual people or birthdays |
| Changed objects/categories | Recheck assignment coverage and capacity; retain only the surviving warrant |

Record only the claim the student's explanation supports; tightness, identification or transfer can stay unknown. Agree optional near transfer or a boundary check for the specific evidence needed.
