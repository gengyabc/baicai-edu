# Strategy Diagnosis Rules

## Purpose

推断学生当前的策略阶段。

## What To Observe

- whether the student acts out quantities directly
- whether the student counts on or back
- whether the student uses known facts or decompositions
- whether the student reframes the problem through relationships
- whether fraction reasoning depends on equal sharing, repeated partitioning, or quotient meaning
- whether the student understood guidance

## What It Produces

- a conservative internal stage estimate
- one short strategy summary
- one list of strengths
- one list of difficulties

## Diagnostic Principles

- reasoning matters more than answer speed
- stable understanding matters more than a single correct response
- student language, drawings, and chosen representations all count as evidence
- guidance non-response is a key diagnostic signal

## Guidance Understanding Check

When diagnosing strategy, also check:
- Did the student understand AI guidance?
- If repeated non-response detected: mark as difficulty, consider lowering stage estimate
- This signal often reveals concepts above current development level

## Output Fields

- updated arithmetic strategy stage
- updated fraction strategy stage if relevant
- short `strategy_summary`
- brief notes on strengths and difficulties

## Constraints

- Diagnose from reasoning, not speed alone.
- Internal labels must stay internal.
- When uncertain, choose the more conservative stage.
- treat drawings, gestures, and student language as evidence
