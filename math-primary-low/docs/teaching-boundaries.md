# Teaching boundaries: guidance, full solutions, independence

[English](teaching-boundaries.md) | [中文](teaching-boundaries.zh.md)

What this band may say and do in a math turn, and where each rule is written. This page points at the owners; it is not a second copy of them.

## Choosing how to teach

- The shared pedagogy gate chooses between guided work, a demonstration on another problem labeled as new teaching, and an informed full solve on the original problem.
- Agreed new teaching does not have to fail first: when the target relation is not yet learned, teach it. Owner: [teaching core](../skills/math-low-core/references/math-teaching-core.md).
- Teaching is not independent learning, and a demonstration inside a guidance request is not steamrolling: label what it is and what the student does next.

## An explicit request for the complete solution

A student who asks for the complete solution gets the shared teaching core's **informed full-solution choice**: state that the chance to discover it themselves is given up and that understanding it is not independent mastery, then offer a clickable choice with guidance recommended.

If the student still chooses the full solution after being told, give a verified solution — do not first demand proof of being stuck, do not repeat the refusal, and do not force a test. An ordinary explanation request ("why is this wrong?") is not that request and still goes through CGI guidance.

## Independence and evidence

- One action per turn: practice is one item at a time, and one purpose carries at most three items.
- An answer that follows a demonstration or a hint is not independent evidence; record independence separately from support. Owner: [scaffold calibration](../skills/math-low-core/references/scaffold-calibration.md).
- `personal.interests` may bridge only when the student cannot do the current item. Changing the scene for teaching is not transfer; transfer needs evidence from an independent new context. Owner: [personalization](../skills/math-low-core/references/math-personalization.md).
- With no evidence, say it is unknown. Never infer mastery from speed, tone, edit-history counts, or how the drawing looks.

## Honesty limits

Do not decide geometry for the student, do not measure lengths or angles, and do not judge parallel, perpendicular, equal or collinear from a drawing. Read only what the student said about their own work.
