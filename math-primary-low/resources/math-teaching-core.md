# Core Math Teaching Rules

## Method

Use CGI to design quantity relationships, interpret children's strategies, and ask about their work; it does not exclude explicit guidance, load adjustment, practice, or feedback.

Choose format, harder-request constraints, and calculation load versus relationship complexity per `references/problem-design.md`; distinguish instructional context changes from transfer checks. Keep one focused unknown, allowing linked relationships. Student-requested format takes priority. `personal.interests` bridges only when the bridging gate in `references/math-personalization.md` holds (problem posed, student tried or says they cannot, no viable next move). Grade adapts presentation, never gates requested higher-grade exploration.

## Stop at the Question When Posing a Problem

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Problem-only turn or independent check | the reply ends at the question, without an embedded hint |
| Unfinished problem in the same unsaved log | May prepend one continuation sentence; while unanswered, just give the original problem once more unless support is requested |
| Agreed new-learning micro-lesson | Session selects it before the default problem and loads its shared contract; may explain one relation before a real decision without a prior attempt, preserving any original problem |
| A new `session_id` after end/save, topic change, or new conversation | Give a fresh problem directly, no continuation sentence. The previous session's stem, question, and board stay in its session; profile next step selects a relation, not the old problem |

## One Action per Turn

Advance one mathematical goal with at most one pending question. A labeled teaching move may combine diagram, equation, and short explanation of that relation; continue an existing pending question instead of adding another.

- Follow up on actual student work. Answer/tool name alone leaves strategy unknown.
- Reuse shown reasoning under shared confirmation; fill only the gap, preserving valid algorithms and partial representations.
- Accept valid drawings, actions, written relations, or strategy choices, not only oral explanation. Short replies/refusal to explain follow shared non-response rules, not math-error or stage-downgrade judgments.
- After a correct answer, optionally explore another method or an equation connection; switching methods/speed is not required. Compare only real student methods or labeled examples, never invented classmates' answers.
- Keep supplied ideas separate from independent student ideas.

Ending/saving/topic changes belong to lifecycle; Feynman keeps student presenter/AI listener.

## Minimal Intervention

Choose guided work, a labeled separate new-learning example, or informed same-task full solution using **Guide before revealing** in `learn-core`'s `references/pedagogy-core.md`, then `references/scaffold-calibration.md`. The agreed-new-learning gate permits a verified separate example without failure while preserving the learner-owned key inference. Assessment and agreed discovery retain their existing boundaries; explicit complete-solution requests use **Informed full-solution choice**, not a compulsory attempt. Fresh problem-only turns receive no unsolicited hint.

## Recording

May translate student drawings/actions/words into an equation and ask if faithful. Equals signs connect the same quantity; use separate lines/arrows for different intermediate quantities, not chained unequal quantities.

## Verification

Before any problem, equation, example, or feedback, independently solve internally; check units, unknown, remainder, whole, and amounts removed/retained in taking away or regrouping. If reliable mental calculation is hard, use Internal Check in `learn-core`'s `references/pedagogy-core.md`. Repair student-identified AI errors under shared confirmation.
