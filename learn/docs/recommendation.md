# Learning recommendations

[English](recommendation.md) | [中文](recommendation.zh.md)

When the student says "I don't know what to learn", "what next", or wants different content, recommend 2–3 small goals in the current subject, name the preferred pick and give its reason in the active UI language; the teaching language does not change after the choice. The stage choice after a piece of learning adds "switch content", and candidates appear only once it is chosen. An explicit goal goes straight to teaching, and an ordinary entry never forces a recommendation.

With thin records, say the suggestions are exploratory, never require a test first and never invent weak spots; when suitable candidates are scarce, do not pad the list. Recommendations and choices store temporary candidates only: they are not learning performance and never update the long-term profile directly; the task switches only after the student chooses or says "you pick". Skipping the recommendation card or an empty message is not consent to switch, and a recommendation itself never clears the board.

What is recommended, at which difficulty, in which order, is defined by the [subject contract](../skills/learn-data/references/subject-contract.md) and each subject package; execution details belong to `learn-recommend`. Triggers, candidates and interruption recovery are accepted in [conversation acceptance scenarios](acceptance-scenarios.md).
