# Progression Map

## Arithmetic Thinking Progression

1. `direct_modeling`: act out with objects, fingers, or drawings
2. `counting_strategy`: count on, count back, or count to a target
3. `derived_facts`: use known facts, doubles, make-ten, and decompositions
4. `flexible_relational`: reframe through inverse relations or part-whole relations
5. `structure_extension`: extend into place value, grouping, and multidigit structure

## Fraction Progression

Use the same four stage names as `profile.current_levels.fraction_strategy_stage`:

1. `equal_sharing_no_coordination`: equal sharing with incomplete coordination
2. `equal_sharing_nonanticipatory`: trial-and-error or repeated halving
3. `equal_sharing_additive`: additive coordination of sharers and shares
4. `equal_sharing_multiplicative`: multiplicative coordination and fraction-as-quotient

After stage 4, move to comparison, equivalence, and operations grounded in quantity relationships; that is the next instructional focus, not a fifth schema value.

## Task Selection Rule

- Use the student's current stage and recent session summaries to choose the next target.
- Prefer the next small step, not the full next unit.
- Revisit fragile ideas before introducing a new abstraction.
