## Primary evidence

1. **Carpenter, Fennema, Franke, Levi & Empson**, [How to Begin Using Cognitively Guided Instruction](https://www.heinemann.com/blog/how-to-begin-using-cognitively-guided-instruction), Heinemann, 2023, fetched in full. When posing problems teachers “do not demonstrate a strategy”; students receive tools and choose meaningful strategies. Later the authors qualify explicit strategy instruction as absent **“in most cases”**, not universally. Organization may vary; teachers adapt numbers and problems. Word, symbolic and other formats are legitimate. Curriculum is **integrated**, unlike addition-before-subtraction or learning skills before using them. This supports nonhierarchical domain access and numbers/tools adaptations, not a mandatory teaching script.
2. **Jacobs & Empson (2016)**, [Responding to children’s mathematical thinking in the moment](https://cadrek12.org/resources/responding-childrens-mathematical-thinking-moment-emerging-framework-teaching-moves), ZDM 48, 185–197, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-015-0717-0). The NSF project/CADRE primary research abstract identifies four categories: ensuring understanding of the story, exploring details of the existing strategy, **encouraging other strategies**, and connecting thinking to notation. Enactment depends on the situation. The retrieved abstract establishes these broad categories; it does not independently verify the exact five-item tool/easier-numbers list now attributed to this paper.
3. **Susan Empson**, [Making Sense of Fractions](https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/blog/making-sense-fractions-susan-empson-phd), full first-person interview transcript, 2026-07-30 rerelease. Children can solve equal-sharing stories **without formal fraction names/symbols**; names attach gradually. However a foundational concept is the size of a piece **relative to its whole**, elicited by “How many of those pieces fit into the whole?” rather than counting all pieces. Equal-sharing stories are useful at the beginning **and throughout** instruction, including equivalence and operations. Special multiplication/division problems with unit fractions can be used early. Number combinations, not merely number magnitude, determine accessibility. She explicitly describes symbolic follow-up equations tied to students’ own reasoning.
4. **UCLA Center X**, [CGI Foundations](https://centerx.gseis.ucla.edu/event/cgi-foundations-k-6th-grade/), official 2024 institute page, fetched in full: participants receive a **“certificate of completion reflecting the total number of hours”**. This is evidence of training completion, **not** product/AI-skill compliance certification. Searches of primary author/UCLA sources did not establish any universal formal CGI compliance certification. Absence of a found scheme is not proof none exists anywhere.
5. **Florida State University project TiPS**, [Place-Value Concepts](https://teachingisproblemsolving.org/place-value-concepts), full page, and [project provenance](https://teachingisproblemsolving.org/about). It treats multidigit reasoning as multifaceted, distinguishing conceptual structures and methods children devise, and cites Carpenter/Fennema-related multidigit research. Supports separate attention to tens/ones reasoning; does not establish that the package’s exact three enum labels constitute an exhaustive universal CGI hierarchy.

## Findings and disposition

### Concrete regression: fraction naming immunity is too broad

[Current fraction-relations:7](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/fraction-relations.md#L7>) says successful sharing but inability to name is an expression gap, **“不算概念错误”**. HEAD limited this to **“不算分配概念错误”**. Valid allocation and fraction-unit understanding are not equivalent. In [teaching-scenarios:37](<../../math-primary-low/tests/teaching-scenarios.md#L37>), two cakes shared among three children produce two thirds per child; naming this 2/6 because six pieces were cut across both cakes may expose a wrong reference whole, not simply missing vocabulary. Empson’s piece-to-whole evidence directly supports retaining the allocation success while probing the unit; do not immunize all naming failures from conceptual diagnosis. Restore the allocation qualifier and explicitly leave unit misconceptions open until clarified. This is a meaningful assessment regression, not a preference for teacher exposition.

### Classification/documentation risks, not established hard CGI violations

- [Progression-map:7](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/progression-map.md#L7>) merges recalled facts into `derived_facts`. A coarse local enum can be legitimate, but recall alone must not become evidence that the student derived a relation. Preserve recall-versus-derivation in `strategy_summary`, and state this is a local aggregate, rather than implying literal identity. The fetched primary sources do not settle the exact canonical label taxonomy; do not claim a verified book quotation or mandatory fourth stage.
- [Progression-map:8–11](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/progression-map.md#L8-L11>) presents exact multidigit/equal-sharing tracks as “CGI order.” Treat these as scoped descriptive aggregates, not a universal ladder. “After” multiplicative sharing, comparison/equivalence/operations are the “next focus” is ambiguous against [fraction-relations:6](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/fraction-relations.md#L6>) and Empson’s early/integrated access. Change to “can be explored throughout; later evidence may emphasize …”. Current explicit non-gating rules prevent classifying this ambiguity as a definite access prohibition.
- [Teaching-core:33](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/math-teaching-core.md#L33>) / [scaffold-calibration:7](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/scaffold-calibration.md#L7>) prohibit demonstrations even on request. More restrictive than the authors’ “in most cases,” but the core explicitly calls these package-specific constraints. Not independently a CGI violation; clarify local scope and preserve strategy comparison/notation discussion. Do not equate CGI with “teacher never supplies anything.”

## Supported improvements and permitted local constraints

- [Grade-defaults:19–29](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/math-grade-defaults.md#L19-L29>): initializing unobserved strategies as unknown and removing global arithmetic prerequisites for multiplication, fractions and multidigit work is consistent with evidence-led integrated instruction.
- [Scaffold-calibration:5](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/scaffold-calibration.md#L5>) / [problem-design:9–11](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/problem-design.md#L9-L11>): allowing tools and adapting numbers repairs the previous blanket bans. Returning to the original numbers and separate supported-performance records are legitimate local controls. Exact number-pair menus/return rules should not be advertised as universal CGI requirements.
- A separate multidigit record is a useful correction to the old global `structure_extension` endpoint, provided actual unit reasoning—not the surface presence of objects or digits—determines interpretation.
- One-action turns, no initial unsolicited hint, default stories, mandatory context changes, exclusion of interests except bridging, and no demonstrations are local product constraints; sources do **not** establish them as CGI mandates. Their mere existence is not grounds for failing a CGI alignment review. Similarly, equal-sharing stages must not subsume all fractions/decimals.

## Book verification (docs/refs/)

### Arithmetic number-fact stage — confirmed local aggregate, keep recall/derivation split

Book 1 Ch.3: three developmental levels are Direct Modeling → Counting → **Number Fact** strategies. Within Number Facts, **Derived Facts** and **Recall** are distinct; Derived Facts usually appear before full recall mastery; fast answers must not be assumed to be recall; Derived Fact and Recall “cut across” levels in Fig. 3.13. Package `derived_facts (includes recalled facts)` is a legitimate local umbrella for the third level, provided `strategy_summary` still separates derivation from recall — which [progression-map:11](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/progression-map.md#L11>) already states. Do not treat the enum label as literal identity with book “Derived Facts.”

### Equal-sharing four stages — book-matched

Book 2 Ch.1 strategy framework, in order of coordination: **No coordination** → **Nonanticipatory** (repeated halving / trial-and-error) → **Additive Coordination** (one item at a time; then groups of items) → **Multiplicative Coordination** (fraction as quotient). Package values `equal_sharing_no_coordination` / `_nonanticipatory` / `_additive` / `_multiplicative` match this framework. Additive “groups” and less-common ratio strategies are finer grain than the enum; keeping them in `strategy_summary` is fine.

### Fraction naming / unit — current text matches book; prior regression disposition stands

Book 2 “Naming Fractions”: children can solve/share without fraction terms; naming is taught via **“How many of these parts fit into the whole?”**, not “how many pieces was it cut into,” because size-relative-to-whole determines the name. Wrong names (e.g. every piece called “a half,” or counting all cuts across items) can be unit/reference-whole misconceptions, not mere missing vocabulary. Current [fraction-relations:7](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/fraction-relations.md#L7>) (“不算**分配**概念错误” + probe which whole) and [teaching-scenarios:41](<../../math-primary-low/tests/teaching-scenarios.md#L41>) (3 share 2 → write 2/6) align with the book. No further wording change required on this axis unless HEAD still lacks the allocation qualifier.

### Multidigit three-value track — scoped descriptive aggregate

Book 1 Ch.7 trajectory: **Direct Modeling / Counting with Ones** (no base-ten use) → **Direct Modeling with Tens** (including partial use, trading/flexibility) → **Invented Algorithms** (Incrementing, Combining Same Units, Compensating). Package `counting_by_ones` / `modeling_tens_ones` / `invented_strategies` collapses ones-level modeling+counting and collapses invented subtypes — acceptable local enum if not advertised as exhaustive CGI ladder. Book also cautions against mastering “easy” number classes before harder ones (regrouping misconceptions); package non-gating rule is consistent.

### Demonstration / explicit strategy — package local constraint, not a book absolute

Book 1 Ch.5: when posing, teachers **“do not demonstrate a strategy”**; tools are offered; students choose meaningful strategies; curriculum is integrated. Qualifier: teachers “**in most cases** do not provide explicit instruction on problem-solving strategies.” Current [teaching-core:31](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/math-teaching-core.md#L31>) / [scaffold-calibration:7](<../../math-primary-low/skills/math-core/references/scaffold-calibration.md#L7>) (no unsolicited demo; labeled example only on explicit request after attempt) is stricter than “in most cases” but compatible with the posing rule and already marked as package convention. Equal-sign chain ban (`6+4=10+3=13`) is directly supported by Book 1’s Colette notation discussion.

No product/AI “CGI certification.” Exact Jacobs & Empson five-item scaffold menu remains secondary-source (abstract + package text), not re-derived from these two books.
