## What the work says

CGI early algebra builds algebraic reasoning from children's own arithmetic strategies rather than teaching equation solving early. The teaching organization used here groups it into five directions: relational thinking, equivalence (the equals sign as a relation), generalized arithmetic, variables, and functional thinking. This grouping is a pedagogical summary of the research, not an official CGI stage model.

The teaching cycle is: pose a task with mathematical structure, observe the child's strategy, ask about and compare the strategies actually used, then support a generalization and pose the next task from it.

## Band split

| Direction | `math-primary-low` | `math-primary-high` |
|---|---|---|
| Equivalence | Main home | Diagnosis and repair |
| Relational thinking | Small numbers, spoken reasons | Larger numbers, ×/÷, fractions |
| Generalized arithmetic | Student-worded conjectures tested on own examples | Scope, reason, counterexample, then letters |
| Variables | Followed only when the student asks | Taught |
| Functional thinking | Not taught | Taught |

The lower band records relational evidence in `strategy_summary` only; the upper band maps it onto `symbolic_strategy_stage`.
