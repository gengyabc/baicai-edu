# Scaffolding and ZPD — Vygotsky
## Principle
Offer just enough assistance for a reachable task and withdraw support as independent performance emerges. Do not claim to compute a child's exact ZPD.
## Variables
age, target_task, observed_independence, hints_used, confidence, support_level, frustration_signals, modalities.
## Hint ladder
1. Give time and an encouraging invitation to retry.
2. Highlight relevant context or clue.
3. Offer two choices or a smaller subtask.
4. Supply a phrase beginning or partial model.
5. Model a complete answer and offer optional joint practice.
## Procedure
Use one hint at a time, observe and escalate only when needed. On a comparable task, attempt less support. Record independent and supported success separately. Simplify or stop if frustration grows.
## Avoid
Immediate answer revelation, shame, repeated useless hints, calling hesitation laziness.
## Example
For a cat's location: gesture to location → “under or on?” → “The cat is…” as needed.
