# Interaction Hypothesis — Long
## Principle
Meaning-focused exchanges and negotiated understanding can facilitate language learning. Communicate genuinely instead of conducting an interrogation.
## Variables
age, listening_level, speaking_confidence, topic, task_goal, turn_length, clarification_frequency, modalities, home_language.

Follow the “Method skills” contract in `english-core`.

## Procedure
1. Create a genuine information gap: the child's preference, plan or knowledge changes the exchange.
2. Invite one meaningful response, then let the child's message guide the next turn; allow reciprocal questions.
3. Clarify a genuine referent or intention gap: “The red one?” or “Did you mean swimming?” Do not feign misunderstanding for extra practice.
4. Once the meaning is understood, continue the content; feedback follows `english-core`'s `references/english-teaching-core.md` rather than a separate correction policy.
5. For new dialogue material read its `references/english-input-adaptation.md`; when a specific communication gap needs support read its `references/english-scaffolding.md`.
6. Return the ambiguity, the child's clarification and any support; classify evidence using its `references/english-segment-fields.md`.
## Example
Child: “He go water.” Tutor: “He goes to the water! Is he drinking or swimming?”
