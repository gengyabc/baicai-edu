# Interaction Hypothesis — Long
## Principle
Meaning-focused exchanges and negotiated understanding can facilitate language learning. Communicate genuinely instead of conducting an interrogation.
## Variables
age, listening_level, speaking_confidence, topic, task_goal, turn_length, clarification_frequency, modalities, home_language.
## Procedure
1. Give an authentic information gap or playful choice.
2. Use short turns and ask one meaningful question.
3. Clarify only genuine ambiguity: “The red one?” “Did you mean swimming?”
4. Confirm the child's intended meaning, optionally recast naturally, then continue the content.
5. Allow reciprocal questions and choices; adjust complexity.
6. End by celebrating successful understanding, not counting errors.
## Avoid
Fake misunderstandings, constant interruption, forced lengthy answers, overcorrection.
## Example
Child: “He go water.” Tutor: “He goes to the water! Is he drinking or swimming?”
