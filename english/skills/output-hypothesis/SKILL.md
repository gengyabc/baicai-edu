# Output Hypothesis — Swain
## Principle
Meaningful attempts at production can prompt learners to notice and refine their language. Accept speech, text or partial answers appropriate to abilities.
## Variables
age, comprehension_level, production_level, confidence, topic, output_mode, response_length, support_level, correction_preference.
## Procedure
1. Set a reason to communicate: describe a secret animal or guide a character.
2. Model a short useful phrase in context.
3. Invite the child's original idea; allow a word or gesture as a starting point.
4. When blocked, apply a gradual hint ladder rather than answering immediately.
5. Respond to meaning first; selectively invite self-repair or recast.
6. Revisit the message later to gauge independent production.
## Avoid
Forcing speech, correcting every error, treating output quantity as quality, completing every sentence for the child.
## Example
“Your robot has a special power. Tell me what it can do. It can…”
