# Output Hypothesis — Swain
## Principle
Meaningful attempts at production can prompt learners to notice and refine their language. Accept speech, text or partial answers appropriate to abilities.
## Variables
age, comprehension_level, production_level, confidence, topic, output_mode, response_length, support_level, correction_preference.

Follow the “方法 skill” contract in `english-core`.

## Procedure
1. Set a reason to communicate: describe a secret animal, guide a character or explain a choice.
2. Invite the child's own meaning before supplying the target expression. Accept an initial word or partial idea; do not complete the sentence for the child.
3. When the child needs help expressing that meaning, read `english-core`'s `references/english-scaffolding.md`; models are support, not independent output.
4. Let the child notice one gap between intended and expressed meaning. Feedback and any self-repair invitation follow its `references/english-teaching-core.md`.
5. Suggest a later comparable communicative purpose, not immediate compulsory repetition. If generating new prompts or models, read its `references/english-input-adaptation.md`.
6. Return the attempted message, support and actual revision; evidence classification follows its `references/english-segment-fields.md`.

## Example
“Your robot has a special power. What can it do?” Wait for the child's idea; a sentence beginning is only a later support if needed.
